## 2. Reddit — r/Living_in_Korea, r/korea, r/koreatravel

**Title:** I made a free site that tells you how to sort trash in Korea from a photo (10 languages, no app)

**Body:**
Sorting trash here confused every foreign friend I know — chicken bones aren't food waste, broken glass isn't recycling, and the rules change by city.

So I built a small free site: take a photo (or type what it is), and it tells you which of the four categories it goes in (general bag / food waste / recycling / special), the steps, and the same steps in Korean so you can show your building manager.

- 10 languages, picks yours automatically
- Multiple items in one photo
- Emergency tab: 112 / 119 / 1330 with the Korean sentence to say, read aloud
- Free, no sign-up, no ads

https://free-in-korea.foreigners.workers.dev

It's AI-based, so it can be wrong — if it gets something wrong, please tell me what you photographed and I'll fix it. Feedback very welcome.
